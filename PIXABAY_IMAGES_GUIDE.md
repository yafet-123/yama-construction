### 4. Image Requirements
- **Format:** JPG or PNG
- **Size:** At least 800x600 pixels
- **Orientation:** Landscape preferred
- **Quality:** High resolution for clear display

### 5. Download Settings on Pixabay
1. Click on the image you want
2. Click "Free Download" button
3. Choose size (recommended: 1280x720 or larger)
4. Save to your computer
5. Rename according to the list above
6. Copy to `public/images/products/` folder

### 6. Alternative: Use Placeholder Images
If you don't have images ready, the website will display a placeholder with the product name and brand. You can add images later without changing any code.

## Quick Start:
